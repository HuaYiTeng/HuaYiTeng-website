FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Buyer FAQs

Direct answers to common procurement questions on MOQ, lead times, custom specifications, compliance, and warranty terms.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ starts from 100 pcs and varies by model and configuration. Some special custom designs require a higher quantity. Send the required model, plug, output and customization details for the applicable MOQ.

What are your sample and bulk production lead times?

For standard products, samples take 3–5 calendar days and bulk production takes 15–20 calendar days. For customized products, samples take 5–10 calendar days and bulk production takes 20–30 calendar days. Timing starts after payment and specification confirmation; international transit is additional.

Which power adapter types and power ratings do you offer?

HYT offers wall-mount, desktop, interchangeable-plug, USB charger, and IP44 waterproof power adapter options with outputs up to 180W. Available voltage and current combinations depend on the series and rated power.

Can you customize the electrical specifications, plug, cable, and branding?

Yes. HYT supports OEM and ODM customization of output specifications, AC plug options, DC connectors, cables, logos, labels, and retail packaging. Feasibility and any impact on safety certification are confirmed for each project.

Which certificates do you provide and how do you check quality before shipment?

HYT holds ISO 9001:2015 factory certification and provides ETL, FCC, CE, UKCA, RCM, PSE, KC, and CCC compliance documentation for applicable models. Every adapter undergoes 100% burn-in testing, specification verification, and batch traceability before shipment.

What is the warranty period?

Most HYT power adapters have a 2-year warranty. The applicable period and terms depend on the specific model and are confirmed prior to order placement.
