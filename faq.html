01. Sample Orders & Delivery

MOQ, lead times, payment terms, repeat orders, and accurate quotation parameters.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ starts from 100 pcs and varies by model and configuration. Some special custom designs require a higher quantity. Send your required model, plug, output and customization details to our team via the website inquiry form for the applicable MOQ.

Can I order samples before a bulk purchase?

Yes. Please contact us via our website inquiry form to confirm technical specifications, then request 1–3 paid samples for engineering and quality evaluation. Sample cost, freight, and production scheduling for your selected model will be confirmed prior to sample dispatch.

For standard products, samples take 3–5 calendar days and bulk production takes 15–20 calendar days. For customized products, samples take 5–10 calendar days and bulk production takes 20–30 calendar days. Timing starts after payment and specification confirmation; the production schedule is confirmed for each order. International transit is additional.

Which payment methods and payment terms are available?

Our standard payment term is 100% advance payment for samples and initial trial orders, and standard commercial terms (such as 30% deposit and 70% before shipment) for approved bulk orders. We accept direct T/T (bank wire transfer) and PayPal. The specific payment method, currency (USD, EUR, CNY), and bank details are subject to the official Proforma Invoice (PI) confirmed by both parties.

How are shipping costs and delivery arrangements confirmed?

Provide your destination country, delivery address or port, quantity, and required arrival date. Yes. Please provide the quantity for each model and AC plug version, plus any expected repeat orders or annual forecast. We can review a tiered quotation against that purchasing plan. Unit price and MOQ depend on the confirmed specifications, packaging, and order conditions.

Can I reorder the same adapter configuration?

Yes. Hua Yi Teng assigns strict internal project codes and BOM specifications. Send your device or application scenario, output voltage and current, AC plug type or input cord, DC connector dimensions and polarity, cable length, destination market, quantity, and customization needs. Include sample requirements and required delivery dates. A label photo, mechanical drawing, or technical datasheet helps our engineers match the optimal solution immediately.

02. Adapter Selection & Replacement

Output voltage, current ratings, DC barrel dimensions, international sockets, and IP44 outdoor safety.

Q09

How can I check whether an adapter will work with my device?

Check your device's required output type (AC or DC), voltage (V), current capacity (A or mA), connector dimensions (OD × ID × length), and polarity (center-positive or center-negative). Share the device input label, original adapter nameplate, and connector drawing with our engineering team for validation.

Q10

Which power adapter types and power ratings do you offer?

HYT offers wall-mount plug-in adapters (3W–72W), desktop adapters (12W–180W), interchangeable-plug travel adapters (5W–65W), USB / Type-C GaN fast chargers (10W–100W), and IP44 outdoor raintight power supplies (3W–60W). Available voltage and current combinations depend on the series and rated power; each adapter is manufactured with fixed, tightly regulated output rather than user-adjustable dials.

Q11

Can I choose an adapter with a higher current rating?

For a regulated constant-voltage DC adapter, a higher current capacity is generally acceptable when voltage, polarity, and connector match; the device draws only the current it requires. This rule does not automatically apply to constant-current LED drivers, lithium battery chargers, or devices with proprietary communication protocols. Inrush peak current and overload protection should be evaluated separately.

Q12

How do I confirm the DC plug size and polarity?

Specify the outer diameter (OD), inner diameter (ID), barrel length, and center-positive (+ inside, - outside) or center-negative polarity. Visually identical plugs often differ: 5.5 × 2.1 mm and 5.5 × 2.5 mm are not reliably interchangeable without spring-fork contacts. A dimension drawing, vernier caliper reading, and device label photo help us guarantee zero assembly mismatch.

Q13

Can I use the adapter in another country?

Check the selected model's AC input range and frequency (HYT adapters typically feature wide input 100–240V AC, 50/60Hz), the local physical plug or inlet cord type, and safety compliance documents for your destination market. Share your target country and application so we can deliver the exact safety-certified plug version (US, EU, UK, AU, JP, KR, etc.). A plug adapter alone does not verify electrical safety compliance.

Q14

Can the adapter be used outdoors or near water?

Use an approved model designed for wet or damp locations. HYT's dedicated IP44 outdoor raintight power supply series provides protection against splashing water and rain for holiday lighting, lawn decor, and fountain pumps, but does not allow submersion or standing water immersion. We will confirm the exact sealing gasket, waterproof threaded connector, and mounting instructions for your application.

Q15

Can you help us replace an existing power adapter?

Yes, our engineering team routinely evaluates replacement and second-source requirements. Send the original adapter label, device input specifications, DC connector dimensions, cable length, and target compliance requirements. Matching voltage and wattage alone is insufficient; we validate electrical, transient, ripple, thermal, and mechanical compatibility with physical samples prior to bulk orders.

Q16

Can I extend the DC cable or power several devices from one adapter?

Cable length and copper conductor cross-section (AWG) dictate voltage drop ($V = I \times R$), while branching to multiple loads requires calculating combined operating current and startup inrush peaks. Connector current ratings and individual line fusing also matter. Share your proposed wiring schematic and total load parameters with us before altering cable lengths or splitting output lines.

03. USB-C & Fast Charging Protocols

Type-C GaN chargers, USB Power Delivery (PD), constant-voltage supplies vs battery chargers.

Q17

Does a USB-C connector or GaN design mean USB PD fast charging?

No. A physical USB-C connector or Gallium Nitride (GaN) semiconductor design does not automatically mean USB Power Delivery (USB PD) protocol support. Fixed-voltage USB-C adapters (e.g. fixed 5V, 12V, or 24V for smart home devices) exist alongside dynamic PD fast chargers (5V/9V/12V/15V/20V). Check the charging protocol chip, voltage/current profile list, and E-marker cable requirements. Please specify your target device and power requirements before selection.

Q18

If the connector fits, can a different adapter charge or power my device?

A matching physical plug does not guarantee electrical compatibility. Always verify output voltage, current capacity, polarity/pinout, and protocol handshaking. Connecting an adapter with excessive voltage or reverse polarity can irreversibly burn internal charging circuits and battery protection ICs. Insufficient current capacity causes thermal throttling, failed charging, or reboot loops. If an incompatible adapter was connected, immediately disconnect and inspect device diagnostics.

Q19

Can constant-voltage USB-C adapters with other output voltages be used interchangeably?

Only after rigorous compatibility verification. A fixed 5V, 9V, 12V, or 24V Type-C adapter cannot be casually plugged into an ordinary smartphone or device designed for 5V input—direct high voltage without protocol negotiation will immediately destroy standard 5V electronics. For constant-voltage industrial devices, voltage must match perfectly while current capacity must meet or exceed demands. For battery applications, external CC/CV charge management is mandatory.

Q20

Can a standard power adapter charge a battery directly?

No. A standard constant-voltage (CV) power adapter is not an intelligent battery charger. Direct lithium, lead-acid, or NiMH battery charging requires a specialized multi-stage charging curve (constant-current CC, constant-voltage CV, float charge, and automatic cut-off termination) to prevent thermal runaway, swelling, or fire hazards. Share your battery chemistry, cell configuration (S/P), pack voltage, and BMS cutoff limits so we can supply a compliant battery charger solution.

06. Technical Support, Warranty & Maintenance

2-year manufacturer warranty, surface warmth, restart troubleshooting, coil noise, and 24/7 continuous duty.

Q28

What is the warranty period?

Most HYT power adapters include a comprehensive 2-year manufacturer warranty. The exact warranty period, terms, and RMA replacement support depend on the model and application environment, confirmed contractually before order placement. If you have questions regarding warranty terms, please reach out via our website inquiry form or direct email.

Q29

Is it normal for an adapter to become warm?

Yes, some surface warmth under full load is normal due to physical power conversion efficiency. HYT adapters comply with international touch-temperature safety thresholds (IEC 62368-1). Operate units within specified ambient temperature and load ratings, ensuring free airflow ventilation. If casing temperature exceeds safety perception, emits burning odor, or displays deformation, immediately disconnect power and report to our support team.

Q30

Why does my device fail to start or repeatedly restart?

Disconnect power before diagnosis. Typical causes include: 1) High startup motor/capacitor inrush peak triggering adapter over-current protection (OCP), causing a hiccup restart loop; 2) Excessive voltage drop across long or thin DC cables; 3) Reversed polarity; 4) Loose DC barrel contact. Send both device and adapter label photos plus a brief fault description or video to HYT support; never arbitrarily increase output voltage to compensate.

Q31

What if I hear buzzing or notice interference?

Determine whether the acoustic buzz originates from internal transformer coil resonance or from the powered audio/display equipment. Acoustic coil whining is often triggered by extreme light-load burst mode or pulsed PWM load switching. For noise-sensitive medical, studio audio, or communication applications, inform us during project inquiry so we can provide specialized ultra-low ripple and enhanced EMI/EMC suppression designs.

Q32

Can the adapter run continuously, 24 hours a day?

Yes, provided operating parameters fall within thermal derating guidelines. Industrial-grade continuous 24/7/365 operation requires evaluating ambient room temperature, duty cycle, load ratio, and ventilation. Thermal derating means operating the adapter below 80% maximum rated load in elevated ambient temperatures (e.g. >40°C) or enclosed kiosks to guarantee component lifespan. Never assume a consumer plastic adapter can run at 100% full load inside an unventilated sealed box indefinitely.
